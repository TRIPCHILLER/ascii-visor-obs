**ASCII ⛶ VISOR** is a video filter plugin for [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) that turns your webcam or another video source into ASCII artwork in real time. Choose characters, change colors and density, and tailor the image to your scene. The result appears immediately in the preview and is included in your recording or stream.

For creative facecam layouts, artistic streams, music performances and video experiments.

An ASCII avatar is also an alternative to VTuber-style presentation: keep your live movements and reactions while showing a stylized image instead of a conventional facecam. It offers some visual privacy, but does not guarantee anonymity: your voice, silhouette and scene details may still be recognizable.

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Use |
|---|---|
| **8 built-in sets** | Give your image a different texture with classic ASCII, alternative characters and geometric shapes. |
| **Custom characters** | Enter your own set to create a distinctive visual style. |
| **20 color palettes** | Choose a ready-made style or adjust your own colors. |
| **Saved color styles** | Save up to 20 custom color presets for each filter. |
| **Transparent background** | Make the spaces between characters transparent and overlay the result on a game, video or scene. |
| **Image controls** | Adjust density, contrast, gamma, black and white levels, scale and position. |
| **Works inside OBS** | Control the effect through the standard Filters panel and use it in recordings and streams. |

Density ranges from **30 to 150 characters across**. Character sets and palettes are independent, so you can mix and match them.

## Help

<details>
<summary>Filter not showing up?</summary>

Completely restart OBS and select your video source. Then: **right-click its image in the preview → Filters → + (the plus icon) → ASCII ⛶ VISOR**. If ASCII VISOR is missing from the list, [report an issue](https://github.com/TRIPCHILLER/ascii-visor-obs/issues/new/choose) with your OS and OBS version.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Updating the plugin</summary>

Close OBS, download the current installer using your platform’s button and choose the same installation destination. **You do not need to uninstall the old version:** the new installer updates it in place, replacing the plugin files. OBS scenes and profiles are preserved.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I remove the plugin?</summary>

Close OBS and follow the removal section of the [installation guide for your system](docs/INSTALL.en.md). Only the plugin is removed; OBS and its settings remain.

</details>

## 💻 Compatibility

Windows x64; Linux x86_64 (Ubuntu 24.04 / glibc 2.39 or newer); macOS universal (Apple Silicon and Intel). OBS is installed separately. The builds are tested with OBS 32.2.2. macOS uses an ad-hoc signature; the installer is not notarized. Linux Flatpak requires a compatible OBS runtime. See the installation guide for requirements and known limits.
